📋 Terms & Conditions

  • The inspection charge is adjusted in the final bill if you approve the repair.
  • If you do not approve the repair, only the inspection charge is payable.
  • Repair fees shown are "starting from" prices. The technician confirms the final price before starting work.
  • Spare parts and materials are charged extra at actual cost, with a bill.
  • Emergency or late-night visits (after 9 PM) have an extra charge of ₹150.
  • All repairs come with a 7-day service warranty.
  • Prices are in Indian Rupees (₹) and may change without prior notice.
